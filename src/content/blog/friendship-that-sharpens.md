You become like the people you spend time with. That can be the best news in your life, or a slow warning.

## Comfortable is not the same as good

Some friendships feel easy because nobody asks anything of anyone. Iron sharpening iron is not comfortable. There is friction. A real friend will sometimes say the thing you don't want to hear.

## Three signs of a sharpening friend

- **They tell you the truth**, gently, even when it costs them.
- **They point you to God**, not just to what feels good right now.
- **They celebrate your growth**, instead of feeling threatened by it.

## Be that friend first

It is easy to wish for friends like this. It is better to become one. Ask a friend how they are really doing. Pray for them by name. Follow up next week.

## Find your people

Faith was never meant to be lived alone. A home group, a bible study or even one other believer you meet with regularly can change the direction of your year.

**Today's step:** send a message to one friend who sharpens you and tell them why you're grateful for them.
