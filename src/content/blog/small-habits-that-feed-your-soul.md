Some days leave you full. Others leave you empty, even when nothing bad happened. Often the difference is not what happened to you, but what you fed on.

## 1. Start before the screen

Give the first five minutes of your day to God, not your phone. Read one verse. Say one honest sentence of prayer. That's it.

## 2. Move your body outside

A short walk clears more than your head. Creation has a way of reminding you how big God is and how small most worries are.

## 3. Say thank you, specifically

Name three things you are grateful for today. Be specific: not "my family," but "the way my sister made me laugh this morning."

## 4. Do one quiet kindness

Help someone without telling anyone. Small, hidden acts of love train your heart more than big public ones.

## 5. End with reflection

Before sleep, ask two questions: *Where did I see God today? Where did I drift?* No judgment. Just awareness.

**Today's step:** pick one of these five and do it for seven days in a row.
