Temptation is not a sign that you are failing. Everyone faces it. What matters is what you do in the next few minutes.

## 1. Name it

Say it plainly, even just in your head: *this is temptation.* Naming it takes away some of its disguise. It is harder to drift into something you have already called by its name.

## 2. Look for the exit

Paul promises there is always "a way to escape." It is usually simple and physical: close the app, leave the room, put the phone in a drawer, go for a walk. Don't negotiate. Move.

## 3. Reach out

Temptation grows in isolation. Send a short message to someone you trust: *"Struggling a bit right now, can you pray for me?"* You don't need to explain everything.

## 4. Replace, don't just resist

An empty space gets filled again. Replace the moment with something good: a short prayer, a psalm, a call to a friend, ten push-ups. Give your mind somewhere better to go.

## 5. Get up if you fall

If you give in, don't spiral into shame. Confess it, receive grace, and learn one thing from it for next time. God's faithfulness is not cancelled by your weakness.

**Today's step:** decide now what your "way of escape" will be, so you don't have to think when the moment comes.
